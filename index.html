Research

Minimization of the Energy Consumption of Electric Heating

Final-year thesis, ENIM · Mitacs Globalink Research Internship, Université de Moncton, Canada (May to August 2025) · Defended 12 September 2025

Design of a smart thermostat system for residential electric heating that keeps thermal comfort while reducing energy use. The work combines a literature study of heating control methods, a distributed hardware and software architecture, and a Model Predictive Control (MPC) algorithm tested in MATLAB/Simulink. In simulation, the MPC anticipates thermal needs, modulates heating power and takes advantage of favorable electricity tariff periods.

Supervisors: Prof. Mohsen Ghribi (Université de Moncton) and Mme Nesrine Majdoub (ENIM).

Projects

Smart heating system: hardware and firmware

Mitacs internship and thesis · Embedded systems

Distributed system of ESP32 thermostats with Nextion touchscreens, BME680 environmental sensors, TRIAC-based power boards and a central control panel. Modules communicate over local Wi-Fi using HTTP/JSON with mDNS discovery. Four custom boards were designed in Autodesk Fusion 360. Laboratory tests confirmed reliable data acquisition and transmission to the central panel.

CAN data transmission PCB for an autonomous shuttle

Actia Engineering Services, Fablab ENIM · Jul to Sep 2024

Designed a custom printed circuit board for the CAN data stage of an autonomous shuttle, using Altium Designer, LTspice and Saturn PCB Design for design and analysis.
