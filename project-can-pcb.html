CAN Data Transmission PCB for an Autonomous Shuttle

Actia Engineering Services, Fablab ENIM · July to September 2024

  • Altium Designer
  • LTspice
  • Saturn PCB Design
  • CAN

Overview

Design of a custom printed circuit board for the CAN data transmission stage of an autonomous shuttle, so that embedded systems on the vehicle can communicate reliably and efficiently.

Tools and method

I used Altium Designer for schematic capture and layout, and LTspice and Saturn PCB Design to analyze the circuit.
