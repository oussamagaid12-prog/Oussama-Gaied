Overview

A smart thermostat system for residential electric heating that aims to keep thermal comfort while reducing energy use. It is a distributed architecture of ESP32 thermostats, a central control panel, environmental sensor modules and TRIAC-based power boards, connected over local Wi-Fi with HTTP/JSON and mDNS discovery.

What I did

I designed the hardware and firmware, including four custom boards in Autodesk Fusion 360, and developed a Model Predictive Control algorithm tested in MATLAB/Simulink. In simulation, the MPC anticipates thermal needs, modulates heating power and takes advantage of favorable electricity tariff periods. Laboratory tests confirmed reliable data acquisition and transmission to the central panel. Running the MPC directly on the ESP32 is the next step.
