Research

Minimization of the Energy Consumption of Electric Heating

Final-year thesis, ENIM · Mitacs Globalink, Université de Moncton, Canada · Defended 12 September 2025
Supervisors: Prof. Mohsen Ghribi (Université de Moncton) and Mme Nesrine Majdoub (ENIM)

This work designs a smart thermostat system for residential electric heating that keeps thermal comfort while reducing energy use. It combines a literature study of heating control methods, a distributed hardware and software architecture, and a Model Predictive Control (MPC) algorithm tested in MATLAB/Simulink. In simulation, the MPC anticipates thermal needs, modulates heating power and takes advantage of favorable electricity tariff periods.
