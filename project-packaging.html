Projects / Packaging Machine

Packaging Machine Automation

Bachelor's final project, Sancella, Monastir · March to June 2022

  • Siemens S7-1200
  • TIA Portal
  • GRAFCET
  • GEMMA
  • EPLAN
  • HMI TP1200 Comfort

Overview

Automation of a toilet paper packaging machine. I analyzed the operative part of the machine, designed new electrical schematics for the control cabinet, and studied the control part with the GRAFCET and GEMMA methodologies.

Implementation

The solution was programmed on a Siemens S7-1200 PLC, with an operator interface on a Siemens TP1200 Comfort panel.
